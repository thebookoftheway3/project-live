# Community code of conduct

Project LIVE welcomes participation across backgrounds, abilities, professional roles and lived experiences. Treat people with respect, explain disagreements with evidence and make room for uncertainty and correction. People may challenge the project model without being dismissed for lacking technical vocabulary. Respect names, accessibility needs, privacy and the choice not to share personal experiences.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, intimidation, retaliation and repeated personal attacks are unacceptable. Critique ideas and behaviour rather than demeaning people. Do not pressure participants to share medical information or try unvalidated treatment. Disclose relevant conflicts and avoid misleading clinical claims.

This policy applies to repository issues, PRs, reviews and other project-operated spaces, and to conduct when representing the project. It does not prohibit respectful disagreement, criticism of maintainers or discussion of uncomfortable evidence.

## Reporting and enforcement

The current enforcement contact is `@thebookoftheway3`. There is no established private conduct mailbox or independent enforcement team. For a public conduct problem, request moderation without repeating abusive or personal content. For a sensitive report, post only a request for a private conduct channel and wait for the maintainer to establish one. Do not place private conduct reports in public issues or misuse the vulnerability-reporting channel for unrelated conduct matters. This temporary contact gap limits confidential reporting and must be addressed as the community grows.

The maintainer reviews available evidence, limits disclosure, hears affected people where appropriate and aims to acknowledge reports within seven calendar days; this is not guaranteed coverage. Responses may include clarification, a warning, content moderation, temporary restrictions or removal of project access, proportionate to impact and recurrence. Urgent protective action can precede full review. Explain the decision to affected people where safe and publish only a privacy-preserving summary when useful. Do not retaliate against good-faith reporters.

Ask for reconsideration with additional evidence. A conflicted maintainer should recuse and seek an uninvolved reviewer with the reporter's consent. Currently no independent appeal body exists, including for reports about the sole maintainer; do not promise independent adjudication. Establish willing additional contacts and an appeal path through the [maintainer process](GOVERNANCE.md) before claiming them. Keep sensitive reports private and retain only what is necessary for handling and accountability.
