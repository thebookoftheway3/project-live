## Problem, context and evidence
Separate observations, interpretations and assumptions. Identify evidence provenance, uncertainty and falsification criteria. Do not include sensitive information.

## Alternatives
Include no change and the simplest viable option. Compare operational complexity, fault tolerance, recovery, security, privacy, human attention and measured need. Mark estimates as estimates.

## Decision and rationale
State intended behaviour, boundaries, invariants, ownership and versioned contracts. Record dissent, conflicts of interest and why alternatives were declined. A proposed decision does not authorize execution.

## Consequences and validation
Benefits, costs, migration/reversal path, failure modes, acceptance criteria, planned evaluation and actual evidence (keep plans separate from results).

## Revisit conditions
What observation would invalidate this choice? Record negative results, review date/trigger and follow-up questions.
