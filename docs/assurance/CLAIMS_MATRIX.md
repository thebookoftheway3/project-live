| **C-08:** Failure cannot be silently converted into certainty. For C-04/C-05, deny first; log only permitted metadata. For C-02, legitimate erasure is compatible with immutability of retained records and may invalidate replay. For C-01, C-03 and C-08, UI/advisory influence is an additional hazard even if no software command is delivered; treatment-user exposure needs its own scoped claims before introduction.
